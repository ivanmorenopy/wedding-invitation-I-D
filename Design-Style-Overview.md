## Design Style Overview

The invitation features an **elegant, classic, and sophisticated luxury theme**. It pairs a dark, regal background with gold/champagne accents, ornate filigree framing, delicate line-art mandala motifs, and refined typography.

---

## Color Palette & Hex Values

* **Primary Background:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0A192F` to `#0B132B`)
* *A rich, dark navy blue with a subtle central spotlight vignette effect.*

* **Primary Accent:** Warm Champagne Gold (`#E6CA65` / `#DFC15D`)
* *Used for key text elements, borders, decorative corner swirls, and divider lines.*

* **Secondary Background Pattern:** Subtle Indigo / Translucent Navy (`#152540` at ~20% opacity)
* *Used for the watermark-style floral/mandala graphics in opposite corners.*


---

## Background & Graphical Details

1. **Vignette Background:**
* Deep navy blue hue that is slightly brighter/lighter in the optical center and gently darkens toward the edges and corners.


2. **Double Border Frame:**
* A clean, thin outer rectangle frame inset slightly from the edge.
* An inner thin rectangular frame nested within.


3. **Corner Flourishes / Filigree:**
* Elaborate scrollwork and ornate vintage flourishes anchor all four corners of the frame.
* A small decorative dot sits at each corner junction.


4. **Watermark Mandala Motifs:**
* Large, geometric, lotus/mandala line-art patterns positioned in two diagonal corners (top-right and bottom-left).
* They use a subtle, semi-transparent indigo tone so they remain low-contrast and non-distracting behind the text content.


5. **Dividers & Accents:**
* Two small solid gold circular dots flank the top tagline ("• VEN A CELEBRAR A •").
* A thin horizontal gold line with a subtle downward diamond ornament in the center separates the main name from the event title.
---

## Typography & Hierarchy

1. **Tagline / Intro Text:**
* *Style:* All-caps, clean sans-serif/geometric grotesque with generous letter-spacing (kerning).
* *Color:* Light Champagne Gold.


2. **Primary Focus (Name):**
* *Style:* Elegant, fluid cursive script font (resembling fonts like *Pinyon Script*, *Great Vibes*, or *Alex Brush*) with flowing flourishes on the initial capital letters ('C' and 'A').
* *Color:* Bright Champagne Gold.


3. **Event Title:**
* *Style:* Bold, blocky, highly legible all-caps sans-serif font.
* *Color:* Bright Champagne Gold.


4. **Event Details (Date, Location, RSVP):**
* *Style:* Medium-weight, clean sans-serif in all-caps, with wide line height (leading) for clarity.
* *Color:* Light Champagne Gold.
---

## Styling Guidelines for Recreation

* **Vibe:** Formal, premium, celebration, milestone event (e.g., Sweet 16, 13th Birthday, Quinceañera, Evening Gala, Formal Dinner).
* **Layout Principle:** Symmetrical center-aligned vertical stack.
* **Contrast Rule:** High contrast achieved between dark midnight blue background and light metallic gold typography.
